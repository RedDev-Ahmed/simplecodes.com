Privacy Policy

At Simple Codes, we value your privacy and want you to understand what may happen to information connected with your visit. This policy applies when you browse our website, read tutorials, or contact us. We aim to request only information that is useful for providing and improving the site, and to handle it thoughtfully. Information you choose to send us, such as your name, email address, or the contents of a message, may be used to reply to you, answer a question, or provide a service you requested. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in a general message. When you visit a website, basic technical information may be made available to the site or its hosting provider. This can include your browser and device type, approximate region, the page that referred you, and the date and time of a request. These details help keep the website available, diagnose errors, protect against misuse, and understand which pages are useful. Our hosting or security providers may process such information on our behalf to deliver their services. We do not use this information to try to identify you personally unless that is necessary to investigate a security issue or comply with a legal obligation. Simple Codes may use cookies or similar browser storage when a feature needs them, or when they help remember a preference and improve how the site works. You can review or clear cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may affect some website features. If analytics or other optional tools are added, they may receive information about how pages are used. We will update this policy to explain relevant changes, and you can use available browser controls to limit tracking where supported. We use information for practical purposes: to operate and maintain the website, respond to your requests, improve lessons and navigation, protect the site and its visitors, and meet applicable legal requirements. We do not need your personal information to read ordinary public pages. We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us host, secure, or maintain the website, and only as needed for those tasks. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of Simple Codes and its visitors. We take reasonable steps to protect information against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. No website, online transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so please contact us without delay if you believe information you sent us has been exposed. We keep messages only for as long as they are useful for responding, maintaining appropriate records, resolving issues, or meeting legal obligations, and then take reasonable steps to remove or anonymize them. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that you have provided. You may also be able to object to or limit certain processing. To make a request, contact Simple Codes and explain what information and action you are asking about. We may need to verify your request before responding, and some information may need to be retained where the law requires it. Our website may link to other websites, learning resources, or social platforms. Those services operate under their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or data practices. Please review their policies before sharing information with them. Simple Codes is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly ask children to submit personal information. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal information can contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. We may revise this Privacy Policy when the website, its features, or relevant requirements change. The updated version will appear on this page with a revised date when appropriate. Continued use of the site after a change means you have had the opportunity to review the updated policy; it does not remove any rights provided by applicable law. If you have a privacy question or request, please contact Simple Codes through the contact method provided on our website. We will review your message and respond as reasonably as we can. We hope this policy helps you understand how Simple Codes handles information and protects your privacy. Thank you for visiting our website and using our resources. The information provided here is for general informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice. If you have specific concerns about privacy or data protection, please consult a qualified professional. Just as we strive to provide clear and useful tutorials, we aim to be transparent about our privacy practices. Your trust is important to us, and we are committed to maintaining it through responsible handling of information. By the way, if you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out to us. We value your feedback and are always looking for ways to improve our services and ensure that your experience with Simple Codes is safe and enjoyable. Also read our Terms of Service to understand the rules and guidelines for using our website. Thank you for taking the time to read our Privacy Policy, and we hope you continue to enjoy learning with Simple Codes.
